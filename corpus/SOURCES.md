# Corpus sources and terms: AEMO Quarterly Energy Dynamics (QED) reports

Generated by `scripts/download_corpus.py` from `corpus.toml`; edit that, not this file.
The documents themselves are **not stored in this repository**: `make ingest` downloads each one
from its original publisher into this folder (git-ignored).

All four are AEMO's *Quarterly Energy Dynamics* reports: the National Electricity Market's quarterly
review of prices, demand, generation and batteries.

**Terms.** (c) Australian Energy Market Operator (AEMO). AEMO's
[copyright permissions](https://aemo.com.au/privacy-and-legal-notices/copyright-permissions) allow
anyone to use AEMO Material for any purpose, provided AEMO is accurately attributed as the author.
These reports are AEMO's own work; anything quoted from them should credit "AEMO, Quarterly Energy
Dynamics". Check that page for the current terms before reusing the material.

The reports are intentionally newer than a small local model's training data, so questions about
them can only be answered by searching the library.
